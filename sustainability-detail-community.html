Focus Areas 

Land Acquisition

Land acquisition is vital for our sustainable growth. We focus on wastelands for power generation, creating local jobs and engaging communities early. Our land-lease system benefits landowners, with rentals at 20-25% of land value. We ensure all legal and environmental approvals are in place before construction.

Project Management

At AGEL, the Project Management and Assurance Group (PMAG) ensures efficient project execution with a focus on ESG integration. Our team optimises project management through advanced tools, improving module capacity and using eco-friendly materials.

Operations and Maintenance

At AGEL, our SOP-driven approach to site management ensures efficient operations and maintenance. Our proprietary technology, integrated with the Energy Network Operations Centre (ENOC), centralises activities, reduces costs, and boosts performance.

Adani Green Energy Ltd. (AGEL), India’s largest renewable energy company, is at the forefront of accelerating the clean energy transition. AGEL develops, owns, and operates utility-scale, grid-connected solar, wind, wind-solar hybrid, and storage solutions. It has an operating portfolio of over 15.5 GW, spread across 12 Indian states. AGEL has set a target of achieving 50 GW by 2030, aligned with India’s decarbonization goals and committed to advancing a sustainable future for all.
