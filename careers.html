Adani Green Energy Ltd. (AGEL), India’s largest renewable energy company, is at the forefront of accelerating the clean energy transition. AGEL develops, owns, and operates utility-scale, grid-connected solar, wind, wind-solar hybrid, and storage solutions. It has an operating portfolio of over 15.5 GW, spread across 12 Indian states. AGEL has set a target of achieving 50 GW by 2030, aligned with India’s decarbonization goals and committed to advancing a sustainable future for all.

Recruitment Disclaimer

Beware of Fraudulent Recruiting!

Recently, some people have been receiving fraudulent emails/phone calls/other communication about job opportunities at Adani Group.

These emails request candidates to deposit money with the agency/in their bank account before they can proceed to the next step in the recruitment process.

These emails are not from Adani Group. We will never ask you to deposit money as part of our recruitment/selection process.

If you or someone you know has received one of these emails, please do not respond to it. Adani Group will not be liable for the consequences of anyone falling prey to this false solicitation.
