SDGs Mapped to Our Environmental Priorities

Adani Green Energy Ltd. (AGEL), India’s largest renewable energy company, is at the forefront of accelerating the clean energy transition. AGEL develops, owns, and operates utility-scale, grid-connected solar, wind, wind-solar hybrid, and storage solutions. It has an operating portfolio of over 15.5 GW, spread across 12 Indian states. AGEL has set a target of achieving 50 GW by 2030, aligned with India’s decarbonization goals and committed to advancing a sustainable future for all.
